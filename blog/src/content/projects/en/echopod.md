EchoPod turns your favorite podcasts into interactive bilingual study material: subtitles and playback stay in sync, and guided echoing sessions train your listening and speaking.

## Key features

- AI subtitles that stay in sync with podcast playback
- Bilingual study material generated from episodes you actually like
- Guided echoing sessions for listening and speaking practice

## Availability

Available on the iOS App Store, with an Android alpha program.
