Ketsuin is a web-based input method that lets you type with ninja hand signs.

## How it works

1. Form one of the 12 classic seals in front of your webcam.
2. A YOLOX-Nano model running on ONNX Runtime WebAssembly detects the sign entirely in your browser.
3. A T9 predictive engine maps seal sequences to text.

## Challenge mode

Race hand-seal sequences against a global leaderboard.
